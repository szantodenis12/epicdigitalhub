## 2. What personal data we collect

### 2.1. Data you provide to us directly

**The "Generate My Free Report" form (Audit page):**
- name;
- email address;
- the address of the website you would like us to analyse.

**The "Check Availability" form (Apply page):**
- name;
- business name;
- city;
- field of activity (niche);
- business website;
- estimated budget;
- stated objective.

**The "Send Enquiry" form (available in the footer of the pages):**
- name;
- email address.

**Direct correspondence:** when you email us, call us or contact us through social media, we process the data you include in your message (for example, name, email address, telephone number, job title, company name and the content of the message).

Please do not send us special categories of data through the forms (for example, health data, religious or political beliefs, biometric data). We do not need such data for our services.

### 2.2. Data collected automatically

**Technical data and server logs:** when you access the website, our hosting provider automatically records technical data necessary for the operation and security of the website: IP address, date and time of access, the page requested, browser type and version, operating system and the page from which you arrived on the website.

**Browsing and measurement data (only with your consent):** if you accept analytics and marketing cookies, we collect, through Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel, information about how you use the website: pages visited, visit duration, traffic source (for example, Google search, an advertisement, a social network), device type, approximate region and actions taken on the website, such as submitting a form. Details are set out in the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

If you decline cookies, Google Analytics may receive, through the Consent Mode feature, signals without cookies and without persistent identifiers, used exclusively for aggregated statistical estimates. Meta Pixel does not load at all without your consent.

**Information about form submissions:** when you submit a form, we record as a measurement event only the type of form (audit, application or enquiry), the page language and, for the application form, the selected niche and budget. **Your name, email address, telephone number and website are not sent to Google Analytics or Meta.**

### 2.3. Data received from other sources

To prepare the audit report you request, we analyse public information about the website and business you indicate (the public content of the website, search engine results, the business's public profiles). This information relates mainly to the business, not to natural persons.

When these periods expire, the data is deleted or anonymised, except where retention is necessary for ongoing litigation or to comply with a legal obligation.

## 9. How we protect the data

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:
- encrypted HTTPS connection across the entire website and an HSTS policy;
- access to data limited to the people who need it, with two-step authentication where providers allow it;
- use of providers offering recognised security guarantees;
- data minimisation: we do not send identifying data from forms to analytics or advertising tools;
- periodic deletion of data whose retention period has expired.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing and, where applicable, the data subjects, within the time limits set by the GDPR.

## 10. Your rights

Under the conditions provided by the GDPR, you have the following rights:

- **the right of access** (Art. 15) — to find out whether we process your data and to receive a copy of it;
- **the right to rectification** (Art. 16) — to ask for inaccurate data to be corrected or incomplete data to be completed;
- **the right to erasure** (Art. 17) — to ask for the data to be erased, for example when it is no longer necessary or you withdraw your consent;
- **the right to restriction of processing** (Art. 18) — to ask for processing to be restricted, for example while the accuracy of the data is being verified;
- **the right to data portability** (Art. 20) — to receive the data you provided in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, or to ask for it to be transmitted to another controller;
- **the right to object** (Art. 21) — to object to processing based on legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your particular situation;
- **the right to withdraw your consent** (Art. 7(3)), at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. For cookies, use the "Cookie settings" link in the website footer;
- **the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing** (Art. 22);
- **the right to lodge a complaint** with the supervisory authority.

**How to exercise your rights:** send a request to **contact@epicdigitalhub.ro** or by post to our registered office. We will reply without undue delay and at the latest within one month of receiving the request. This period may be extended by a further two months where the request is complex or we receive a large number of requests, in which case we will inform you within the first month. Exercising your rights is free of charge. If a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, we may charge a reasonable fee or refuse to act on the request. To protect your data, we may ask you for additional information to confirm your identity.
